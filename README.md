## **Governance, Risk and Compliance**

A practical GRC documentation portfolio built around a fictional Swiss company, **Kontrucktors SA**, specialising in the leasing and sales of trucks and construction equipment across Geneva, Lausanne, Zurich, and Lugano.

### **About This Repository**

This repository contains a growing library of information security policies, risk management documents, audit reports, and compliance frameworks, developed as part of an 8-week GRC documentation challenge.

All documents are written to ISO 27001:2022 standards and reference applicable Swiss and European regulations including the Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP/LPD) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The purpose of this portfolio is to demonstrate practical GRC competencies including policy writing, risk assessment, control mapping, and audit methodology, applied to a realistic business context.

### **Document Standards**

All documents in this repository follow these conventions:

* Written to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A control requirements  
* Referenced against GDPR and Swiss FADP/LPD where applicable  
* Include document control metadata: reference number, version, classification, effective date, review date, owner, and approver  
* Use a four-tier data classification scheme: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted  
* Include named roles and responsibilities  
* Fictional company details are used consistently throughout to reflect realistic business context
