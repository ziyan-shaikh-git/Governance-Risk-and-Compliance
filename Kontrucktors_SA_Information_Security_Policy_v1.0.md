**1\. Purpose**

This Information Security Policy (ISP) establishes the overarching framework for managing information security at Kontrucktors SA. It defines the company's commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information assets, and sets out the principles, responsibilities, and minimum standards that apply across all operations.

This policy forms the cornerstone of Kontrucktors SA's Information Security Management System (ISMS) and is aligned with the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP/LPD), and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

**2\. Scope**

This policy applies to all information assets owned, processed, transmitted, or stored by Kontrucktors SA, including but not limited to: customer leasing and sales contracts, fleet management data, employee records, financial information, supplier agreements, and operational technology systems.

It applies to all personnel who access Kontrucktors SA information systems or handle company data, including:

* Permanent and temporary employees across all four office locations  
* Contractors, consultants, and freelance workers engaged by the company  
* Third-party vendors and technology providers with access to company systems or data  
* Board members and executive management

 

The scope encompasses all information processing facilities operated by or on behalf of Kontrucktors SA, whether located on-premises, hosted in the cloud, or accessed remotely from field operations across Switzerland.

**3\. Information Security Objectives**

Kontrucktors SA is committed to achieving and maintaining the following information security objectives:

* Protect the confidentiality of customer, employee, and business data against unauthorised access or disclosure  
* Maintain the integrity of information to ensure it is accurate, complete, and trustworthy  
* Ensure the availability of critical systems and information to support uninterrupted business operations  
* Comply with all applicable Swiss and European legal and regulatory requirements relating to data protection and information security  
* Continuously identify, assess, and treat information security risks in a structured and documented manner  
* Foster a security-aware culture across all offices and business functions  
* Ensure all third parties and suppliers who process company data meet equivalent security standards

 

These objectives are reviewed annually and reported to senior management as part of the ISMS management review process.

**4\. Information Security Principles**

**4.1 Confidentiality**

Information must be accessible only to those individuals who have a legitimate business need and have been formally authorised to access it. Access rights are granted based on the principle of least privilege and are reviewed at regular intervals and upon changes to employment status.

**4.2 Integrity**

Information must be protected against unauthorised modification, deletion, or corruption. Controls including access restrictions, change management procedures, audit logging, and data validation are in place to ensure the accuracy and completeness of company data throughout its lifecycle.

**4.3 Availability**

Information systems and data must be available to authorised users when required. Business continuity and disaster recovery plans are maintained to ensure that critical operations can continue in the event of a disruption, including system failures, cyberattacks, or natural events.

**4.4 Risk-Based Approach**

Information security decisions at Kontrucktors SA are based on a formal and documented risk assessment process. Risks are identified, evaluated, and treated in accordance with the company's risk appetite, and residual risks are accepted only by authorised senior management.

**4.5 Accountability**

All individuals who access or handle company information are personally accountable for their compliance with this policy and all associated security procedures. Responsibility for information security is defined at all levels of the organisation, from the board to individual users.

**4.6 Legal and Regulatory Compliance**

Kontrucktors SA complies with all applicable legal and regulatory requirements relating to information security and data protection, including the Swiss FADP/LPD, the GDPR where applicable, contractual obligations with customers and suppliers, and sector-specific requirements relevant to the leasing and sales of vehicles and construction equipment.

**6\. Information Security Risk Management**

Kontrucktors SA operates a formal information security risk management process aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 6.1. The process comprises the following stages:

**6.1 Risk Identification**

Information assets are identified and documented in the company asset register. Threats and vulnerabilities relevant to each asset are identified through a combination of internal assessments, industry threat intelligence, and review of historical incidents.

**6.2 Risk Assessment**

Each identified risk is evaluated using a likelihood and impact matrix to determine a risk rating. Risk assessments are conducted annually and following any significant change to the company's IT environment, business operations, or threat landscape.

**6.3 Risk Treatment**

Risks are treated through one or more of the following options: implementing security controls to reduce the risk, transferring the risk through insurance or contractual arrangements, avoiding the risk by ceasing the relevant activity, or accepting the risk where it falls within the company's defined risk appetite. All risk treatment decisions are documented in the Risk Treatment Plan.

**6.4 Risk Acceptance**

Residual risks that remain after treatment must be formally accepted by the Chief Executive Officer or a delegated member of senior management. Accepted risks are reviewed at the next scheduled risk assessment.

**7\. Information Asset Management**

All information assets of significant value to Kontrucktors SA must be identified, classified, and assigned an owner. Asset owners are responsible for ensuring their assets are protected in accordance with their classification and this policy.

Information assets are classified according to the four-tier scheme defined in the Acceptable Use Policy (KSA-ISP-AUP-001): Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Classification determines the level of protection required and the controls that must be applied.

* Asset inventories are maintained by the IT Department and reviewed annually  
* Assets must be returned or securely wiped upon termination of employment or contract  
* Physical assets including laptops, mobile devices, and storage media must be tracked and secured at all times  
* Cloud-based assets must be managed through approved platforms only

**8\. Human Resources Security**

**8.1 Pre-Employment**

Background verification checks are conducted for all new employees and contractors in accordance with applicable Swiss law and the sensitivity of the role. All personnel must sign confidentiality agreements and acknowledge this policy prior to being granted access to company systems.

**8.2 During Employment**

All employees and contractors must complete information security awareness training upon joining and on an annual basis thereafter. Additional training is provided when significant changes to the threat landscape or company security requirements occur.

**8.3 Termination and Change of Role**

Upon termination or change of role, access rights must be revoked or adjusted promptly by the IT Department. The HR department is responsible for notifying IT of all terminations and role changes within one business day. All company assets and confidential information must be returned on the final day of employment.

**9\. Physical and Environmental Security**

Physical security controls are implemented at all Kontrucktors SA office locations in Geneva, Lausanne, Zurich, and Lugano to prevent unauthorised access to information processing facilities and sensitive data.

* Access to office premises is controlled through key card or equivalent access control systems  
* Server rooms and IT infrastructure areas are restricted to authorised IT personnel only  
* Visitors must be signed in, supervised at all times, and signed out upon departure  
* Sensitive printed documents must be stored securely and disposed of using cross-cut shredding  
* Clean desk and clear screen policies apply in all office environments  
* Uninterruptible power supplies and environmental controls are maintained in all server rooms

**10\. Access Control**

Access to Kontrucktors SA information systems and data is granted on the basis of business need and the principle of least privilege. Detailed access control requirements are documented in the Access Control Policy (KSA-ISP-003).

Key requirements of this policy include:

* All users must be uniquely identified and authenticated before accessing company systems  
* Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for remote access and all cloud-based applications  
* Access rights are reviewed at a minimum every six months and immediately upon change of role or termination  
* Privileged access accounts are strictly controlled and subject to enhanced monitoring  
* Shared or generic accounts are prohibited unless specifically approved by the Information Security Manager

**11\. Cryptography and Encryption**

Kontrucktors SA applies encryption to protect sensitive information in transit and at rest. The following minimum standards apply:

* All laptops and mobile devices must have full-disk encryption enabled  
* Data transmitted over public or untrusted networks must be encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher  
* Cryptographic keys must be managed securely and rotated in accordance with IT Department procedures  
* The use of deprecated or weak encryption algorithms is prohibited

**12\. Supplier and Third-Party Security**

Third parties who access, process, or store Kontrucktors SA information must meet equivalent security standards to those required of the company's own employees. Prior to engagement, all suppliers handling sensitive data must:

* Complete a security assessment or provide evidence of equivalent certification such as ISO 27001  
* Sign a Data Processing Agreement or equivalent contractual arrangement  
* Agree to comply with Kontrucktors SA's information security requirements as a condition of the contract  
* Notify Kontrucktors SA immediately of any security incidents affecting company data

 

Supplier security is reviewed annually and upon renewal of contracts. The IT Department maintains a register of all approved third-party suppliers with access to company systems or data.

**13\. Information Security Incident Management**

All actual or suspected information security incidents must be reported immediately to the Information Security Manager. A security incident includes any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of Kontrucktors SA information or systems.

The company maintains a formal Incident Response Policy and Procedure (KSA-ISP-005) that defines the steps for detection, reporting, assessment, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident review. All incidents are logged, investigated, and used to improve the ISMS.

Data breaches affecting personal data must be assessed for notification obligations under the FADP/LPD and GDPR. Where notification is required, the relevant supervisory authority must be informed within 72 hours of discovery where practicable.

**14\. Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery**

Kontrucktors SA maintains business continuity and disaster recovery arrangements to ensure that critical information systems and operations can be restored following a significant disruption. These arrangements are documented, tested, and reviewed annually.

* Critical systems and data are backed up daily to secure, geographically separated locations  
* Recovery Time Objectives and Recovery Point Objectives are defined for all critical systems  
* Business continuity plans are tested at least annually through tabletop exercises or live tests  
* All staff are made aware of their roles in the event of a business continuity incident

**15\. Compliance Monitoring and Internal Audit**

Kontrucktors SA conducts internal audits of its ISMS at least annually to verify compliance with this policy, associated procedures, and ISO 27001:2022 requirements. Audit findings are reported to senior management and used to drive continuous improvement.

Non-conformities identified during audits must be subject to documented corrective action plans with defined owners and completion dates. The Information Security Manager is responsible for tracking corrective actions through to closure.

Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements is monitored on an ongoing basis. Any changes to applicable legislation are assessed for impact and implemented within a defined timeframe.

**16\. Policy Review and Maintenance**

This policy is reviewed annually by the Information Security Manager and approved by the Chief Executive Officer. It is also reviewed following any significant change to the organisation, its information systems, or the threat environment. All material changes are communicated to all in-scope personnel, who are required to re-acknowledge the updated policy.
