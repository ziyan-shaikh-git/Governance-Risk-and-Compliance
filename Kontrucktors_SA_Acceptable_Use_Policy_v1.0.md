**1\. Purpose**

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the rules and responsibilities governing the use of Kontrucktors SA information systems, technology assets, and data resources. It is designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of company information and to ensure that all users of Kontrucktors SA systems act in a manner consistent with Swiss law, applicable data protection regulations including the Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP/LPD) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the company's internal security standards.

This policy applies to all information systems owned, leased, or operated by Kontrucktors SA, including but not limited to: workstations, laptops, mobile devices, servers, cloud services, communication platforms, and network infrastructure across all four office locations in Geneva, Lausanne, Zurich, and Lugano.

**2\. Scope**

This policy applies to:

* All permanent and temporary employees of Kontrucktors SA

* Contractors, consultants, and freelancers engaged by Kontrucktors SA

* Third-party vendors and partners with access to Kontrucktors SA systems or data

* Board members and senior management accessing company information resources

This policy covers all information systems, devices, and networks used to conduct Kontrucktors SA business, regardless of whether those systems are located on company premises, at a client site, or accessed remotely.

**4\. Authorised Use of Company Systems**

Kontrucktors SA information systems are provided for legitimate business purposes. Users are expected to use these resources in a professional, lawful, and responsible manner. The following uses are authorised:

**4.1 Business Use**

* Processing customer leasing and sales contracts for trucks and construction equipment

* Managing fleet records, maintenance schedules, and vehicle documentation

* Communicating with clients, suppliers, and partners via approved channels

* Accessing internal business applications and databases required for job functions

* Using approved cloud services and collaboration tools for work-related purposes

**4.2 Limited Personal Use**

Incidental and reasonable personal use of company systems is permitted provided it does not interfere with work responsibilities, does not consume significant company resources, complies with all other provisions of this policy, and does not create legal liability for Kontrucktors SA. Users should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems for personal purposes.

**5\. Prohibited Activities**

The following activities are strictly prohibited on Kontrucktors SA systems and networks:

**5.1 Data Security**

* Accessing, copying, or sharing company data without authorisation

* Transmitting sensitive customer or fleet data via unencrypted or unapproved channels

* Storing company data on personal devices or unauthorised cloud storage services

* Attempting to circumvent access controls, authentication mechanisms, or security software

* Sharing login credentials or access tokens with any other person

**5.2 System Integrity**

* Installing unauthorised software, applications, or browser extensions on company devices

* Modifying, disabling, or tampering with security software, firewalls, or monitoring tools

* Attempting to access systems or data beyond the scope of assigned job responsibilities

* Connecting personal devices to the corporate network without prior authorisation from IT

* Using company systems to mine cryptocurrency or perform non-business computations

**5.3 Legal and Ethical Violations**

* Accessing, downloading, or distributing illegal, offensive, or discriminatory content

* Engaging in harassment, bullying, or any form of unlawful conduct via company systems

* Violating intellectual property rights, including software licensing agreements

* Using company systems to conduct personal commercial activities or competing business

* Misrepresenting identity or impersonating another individual in any communication

**5.4 Network and Communications**

* Using the corporate network to launch attacks against internal or external systems

* Intercepting, monitoring, or recording network traffic without written authorisation

* Connecting unauthorised wireless access points or network equipment

* Using anonymisation tools, VPNs, or proxies to bypass corporate security controls without IT approval

**6\. Password and Access Management**

All users are responsible for maintaining the security of their access credentials. The following requirements apply:

* Passwords must be a minimum of 12 characters and include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters

* Passwords must not be shared, written down in accessible locations, or stored in unencrypted files

* Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote access and cloud application logins

* Users must lock their workstation (Windows \+ L) when leaving their desk unattended

* Access rights are assigned based on job function and the principle of least privilege

* Users who no longer require access to a system must notify IT immediately for access revocation

* Default vendor passwords must be changed before any device is connected to the corporate network

**7\. Device and Mobile Device Use**

All company-issued devices must be used in accordance with the following requirements:

* Devices must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times

* Automatic screen lock must activate after a maximum of 5 minutes of inactivity

* Operating system and application security updates must be applied within 14 days of release

* Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT and management within 2 hours of discovery

* Personal mobile devices used for company business must be enrolled in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) system

* Company data must not be stored locally on mobile devices unless encrypted and authorised

* Devices used to access company systems in field operations across Switzerland must comply with the same security requirements as office-based devices

**8\. Email and Communication Systems**

Users must adhere to the following requirements when using company communication tools:

* Company email accounts must be used for all business communications; personal email accounts must not be used for company business

* Users must exercise caution with attachments and links received from unknown or unexpected sources

* Phishing or suspicious emails must be reported to IT immediately and must not be forwarded

* Bulk or mass communications on behalf of the company require approval from senior management

* Confidential or sensitive information must not be transmitted via unencrypted email

* Auto-forwarding of company email to personal accounts is prohibited

**9\. Data Handling and Classification**

**9.1 Data Classification**

Kontrucktors SA classifies its information into the following categories:

| Public | Approved for external distribution | Marketing materials, product catalogues, website content |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Internal | For internal use only; not for external disclosure | Fleet schedules, internal procedures, staff directories |
| Confidential | Restricted to authorised personnel; requires protective controls | Customer contracts, pricing, financial records, HR data |
| Restricted | Highest sensitivity; access on strict need-to-know basis only | Trade secrets, acquisition plans, legal strategy, executive credentials |

**10\. Security Incident Reporting**

All users are required to report suspected or confirmed security incidents immediately. A security incident includes, but is not limited to: unauthorised access to systems or data, lost or stolen devices, malware or ransomware infection, phishing attacks that were clicked or responded to, and data breaches or unintended disclosures of confidential information.

Incidents must be reported to the IT department or the Information Security Manager by telephone or email immediately upon discovery. Users must not attempt to investigate, remediate, or cover up a security incident independently. Failure to report a known security incident is a disciplinary offence.

**11\. Monitoring and Audit**

Kontrucktors SA reserves the right to monitor, log, and audit the use of all company information systems in accordance with applicable Swiss law and the Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Monitoring activities may include network traffic analysis, email and web access logging, device activity logs, and access control reviews.

Users are advised that they have no expectation of privacy when using company-owned systems or networks. Monitoring is conducted for security, compliance, and operational purposes only. Any evidence of policy violations identified through monitoring may be used in disciplinary proceedings.

See Policy Violations and their consequences below.

**12\. Policy Violations and Consequences**

Violations of this policy may result in one or more of the following actions, depending on the severity and intent of the violation:

* Verbal or written warning

* Suspension or revocation of system access

* Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract

* Civil or criminal referral to the appropriate Swiss authorities

* Recovery of costs arising from the violation

Kontrucktors SA will investigate all reported policy violations promptly and fairly. Users who report violations in good faith are protected from retaliation.

**14\. Policy Review**

This policy will be reviewed annually or following any significant change to the organisation's IT environment, applicable law, or security threat landscape. The Information Security Manager is responsible for initiating the review process and presenting proposed amendments to senior management for approval. All users will be notified of material changes and required to re-acknowledge the updated policy.
